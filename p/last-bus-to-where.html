Last Bus to Where

Some destinations don't exist. Some contracts never end.

Tobi has done the math. Staying in Nigeria means watching his sister die. Leaving could give her a chance.

So when Red Sand Travels offers him a route to Europe with no payment upfront, the offer is impossible to refuse. But Red Sand isn't selling tickets. It's selling debt.

From Lagos to Kano, across the Sahara and into Libya, every kilometer north carries a price, and every promise in the contract means exactly what it says. Among strangers who have become cargo, survival depends on knowing whom to trust, when to obey, and when to break the rules.

The most dangerous question isn't whether Tobi will make it. It's what will still belong to him if he does.
