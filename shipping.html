Shipping & FAQ

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Shipping

  • 🚚 $5.99 standard shipping on orders under $50.
  • 🎉 FREE standard shipping on orders of $50 or more.
  • 📅 Delivery in about 5–8 business days, including the time we need to pack your order.
  • 🇺🇸 We ship to addresses in the United States only.
  • 📍 Orders ship from Denver, Colorado.

You'll see the shipping cost and total before you pay. After you pay, you'll get an email receipt, and we'll email you when your order ships.

Returns

You can return items within 30 days of delivery. If an item arrives damaged, defective or not as described, we cover return shipping or send a replacement. See our full return & refund policy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Click "Buy now with card" on any product. You'll see your order with shipping and total, then pay on a secure card checkout page. We accept major credit and debit cards such as Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover.

Is my card information safe?

Yes. You enter your card on our payment processor's secure, encrypted checkout page. We never see or store your full card number.

When will my order arrive?

Usually within 5–8 business days of your order. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US holidays.

Do you ship outside the United States?

Not at the moment. We only ship to US addresses.

Are the books, records and collectibles new or used?

Each listing says clearly whether the item is new or used/pre-owned. Used items are described honestly, including visible wear, and the photos show the actual item or its exact edition.

My item arrived damaged or isn't what I ordered. What do I do?

Contact us with your name, order details, the reason, and clear photos of the item and any damage. We'll make it right with a replacement or refund, and we cover return shipping in that case.

Can I buy from you on other platforms?

Yes. You'll also find us on eBay (jib1504), TikTok Shop and our Facebook Shop. Orders there follow that platform's buyer protection and return rules.
