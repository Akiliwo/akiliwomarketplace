Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Akiliwo Marketplace LLC ("we", "us") collects when you visit www.akiliwomarketplace.com or buy from us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Akiliwo Marketplace LLC is a small online store based in Denver, Colorado, USA.

Email info@akiliwomarketplace.com or call/text +1 720 799 4089.

Information we collect

  • When you place an order: your name, email address, phone number, shipping address and the items you buy. You enter this on our secure checkout page, which is run by our payment processor.
  • Payment details: your card number is entered directly on the payment processor's secure page. We never see or store your full card number. We only receive confirmation that the payment went through.
  • When you contact us by email, phone, text or social media: your name, contact details and what you tell us, so we can answer you.
  • When you browse our website: technical information such as the pages and products you view, the device and browser you use, and whether you started checkout or completed a purchase. This is collected with cookies and similar tools (see "Advertising and analytics" below).

How we use your information

  • To process, pack and ship your order, and to send you your receipt and order updates.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns and refunds.
  • To prevent fraud and keep our store secure.
  • To understand which products people are interested in and improve our website.
  • To show our ads to people who may be interested in our products and to measure whether those ads work.

Advertising and analytics

Our website uses the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) and may use the Google tag (Google Analytics and Google Ads). These tools use cookies and similar technology to record visits, product views, checkout starts and purchases on our site. Meta and Google may use this information to show you our ads on their platforms and to measure results, in line with their own privacy policies.

You can limit this at any time:

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a browser or extension that blocks tracking.
  • Manage Facebook and Instagram ad settings at facebook.com/adpreferences.
  • Manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Opt out of interest-based ads from many companies at optout.aboutads.info.

Who we share information with

We share only what is needed with:

  • our payment processor, to take your payment securely;
  • shipping carriers, to deliver your order;
  • Meta and Google, through the advertising tools described above;
  • authorities, if the law requires it or to protect against fraud.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

Orders placed on eBay, TikTok Shop, Facebook or other marketplaces are also covered by that platform's own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as needed for shipping, returns, accounting and tax purposes, and as required by law. Messages are kept as long as needed to help you.

Keeping your information safe

Our website uses a secure (HTTPS) connection, and payments are handled on our payment processor's secure checkout. No online service is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's law.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
